# The Little Robot
Once upon a time, in a quiet village by the hills, there lived a little robot named Pico. Every morning Pico watered the garden and greeted the farmers.

One day the village well went dry. Everyone was worried about the crops and the animals.

Pico: I can help! Let me look for water.
Mira: Be careful, Pico. The mountains are far away.

Pico walked for three days and found a hidden river under the old mountain. He called the villagers and together they dug a new canal.

The village was green again. Pico learned that a small friend can solve a big problem when everyone works together.
